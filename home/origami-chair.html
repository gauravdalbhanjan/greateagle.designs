Sustainable Origami Chair

De-clutter your space, de-noise your mind. An origami-inspired chair from bio-based polycarbonate that addresses tariff tensions and plastic waste with minimal manufacturing complexity.

The Problem

Seating arrangements in public spaces can cause social discomfort, anxiety and overwhelming stress due to cognitive load — reducing the joy of a weekend outing. Conventional plastic furniture also carries a heavy carbon and waste footprint.

The Design

Stance uses bio-based polycarbonate from cornstarch, addressing tariff tensions and plastic waste. Its origami-inspired design folds into a backrest and legs with minimal manufacturing complexity. The transparent form suits offices, libraries and exhibitions.

The Impact

  • Delivers 65% less carbon and energy than conventional plastic.
  • Eliminates 90% of waste economically.
  • Could replace $1.4B in plastic imports, create 5,000 jobs, and generate $2.9B in domestic economic impact.
  • Taps into an $81B sustainable plastics market by 2030, positioning the US as a leader in domestic manufacturing and global sustainable exports.

"Stance aims to cut carbon emissions, reduce manufacturing complexity, use locally available material, encourage agriculture and create a circular economy. A chair could be a start, a beginning, a step forward…"
