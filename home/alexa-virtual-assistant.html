Alexa as a Customer Assistant

One assistant, three tiers 📞 → 🖥️ → 🏠. A voice-first service that starts as a plain phone call, grows a screen, then dissolves into the room itself.

The problem

🚧 Not coverage. Use.

90%+ of the unconnected already live under coverage. What stops them is literacy, language, cost and app skills — a phone call sidesteps all of it.

🗣️English: ~19% of speakers, ~61% of the web.
🔤Reading & typing assumed — SMS fails where literacy is low.
💸Data plans & smartphones cost more than many can spend.
👩🏽‍🌾The excluded skew rural, female, older, less-educated.

The tiers

🧩 Depth, not complexity

One channel, three tiers — each contains the one below it.

📞
1 · Base

Audio only, over a call

Any basic phone. Weather, news, scheme advice & alerts — by voice, in the caller's language.

🖥️
2 · Intermediate

A screen & a work window

Launch SaaS apps by voice; a home window beside work; extend a laptop onto the big screen.

🏠
3 · Premium

The interface in your room

Projected and ambient, powered by SaaS apps. Beyondaryless, edgeless — the experience, not the device.

The business model

💰 The call is the channel. Apps are the business.

A catalog of SaaS apps — built from Amazon's startup-cluster tools (Amazon Quick is one) — earns through subscription & revenue share. Base is sponsored; the return is reach, language data, and demand discovery.

📞 Base — Sponsored / grants. No user revenue; ~$0.14/user/mo modeled cost. Return = reach & data.
🖥️ Intermediate — Telco revenue share. A Rs 30–50 add-on ≈ 10–25% of Indian ARPU.
🏠 Premium & SaaS — Subscription + platform share. Per-seat for business apps.
🌍 Opportunity 1 · Reach

The next billion, by voice

Serve the 3.1B the internet skips — through the simplest tech they already own, a phone call. Inclusion as the product.

💼 Opportunity 2 · Revenue

A storefront for SaaS

Turn internal startup-cluster tools into services people find, use & pay for by voice — the assistant as the channel.

The ask

🙋 Fund a Base-tier pilot

One Indian region, one sponsor. Measured on verified containment, cost per resolved call, and demand signals — before building the upper tiers.

📖 What we still need to validate ›
  • 🤝Which sponsor funds the pilot, and which languages launch first?
  • 💸Toll-free or on-device delivery — the single biggest cost lever.
  • 📈Rural willingness to pay, plus retention and upgrade targets.
  • ⚠️Can Premium be built affordably? (Samsung's shelved Ballie says: keep it software-first.)
The experience we're creating

✨ Beyond the screen's edges

Like immersive installation art, the assistant dissolves the boundary between interface and room. Work and home aren't windows trapped in a screen — they live in your environment, projected and ambient. Beyond infrastructure. Beyond device limits.

🧵 Mood board — soft, edgeless, part of the environment.

🎨 CMF variants — the object as furniture, not hardware.

The design

💡 An interface with (almost) no interface

The best constraint: the Base device speaks and shows two colors of light — nothing more. Three moves kept it expressive and on-brand.

🔲 Brand, pixel by pixel

A pixelated Alexa logo that stays readable even rendered by a handful of LEDs — identity that degrades gracefully.

🔢 Dialing as muscle memory

The keypad & alphabet layout are shaped around the physical gesture of dialing — so calling feels natural, not taught.

In the real world

🌾 Rural agriculture

A Base-tier call: a farmer asks about 🌦️ the week's weather and ⚡ the local load-shedding schedule, hears a trusted spoken answer, and gets a 💧 smart-irrigation alert before the next watering window.

☁️ Precision farming on AWS

Not just slides — a working AWS architecture at the AWS Skill Center turns field data into watering schedules. Telephony ☎️ + speech-to-text + a language model 🧠 + Amazon Polly 🔊, one pipeline, 24/7, with data scoped so users only see what they're allowed to 🔒.

🏡 At home & for students

💼 At home

Work & home, one glance

Link a laptop to the home screen as an extension display — dashboards & SaaS workflows beside a home window for chores, ordering, news, CCTV briefing & entertainment.

🎓 Students

Learning anywhere

A dorm lamp projects onto the wall, past the screen into the room; campus labs (College of Engineering, Pune) support AWS training.

Key takeaways

🎯 Three things to take away

  • 1️⃣Audio opens the service to everyone — voice reaches any phone, no screen needed.
  • 2️⃣Each tier adds depth, not complexity — Base, Intermediate & Premium build on one another.
  • 3️⃣Premium is powered by SaaS apps — Amazon Quick and countless start-ups are services waiting to be monetized.
