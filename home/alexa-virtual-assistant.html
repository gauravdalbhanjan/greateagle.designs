The experience we're creating

Infrastructure vs Interface

01 · A tiered service model

🧩 Depth, not complexity

Each tier adds depth, not complexity — Physical, Digital and Spatial build on one another.

📞
1 · Physical

Audio only, over a call

Audio-only over standard phone calls on any basic phone — weather reports, news & audio entertainment, by voice in the caller's language.

🖥️
2 · Digital

A screen & a work window, now business-capable

Hybrid voice-screen. On big screens, step into the business environment — SaaS apps (Amazon Quick is one example) with live workflows, beside the home window (chores, ordering, news, CCTV briefing, Alexa & Alexa Plus).

🏠
3 · Spatial

The interface in your environment

Environment-native, powered by SaaS apps. The same business + home experience, but the interface leaves the screen and lives in the user's space — projected and ambient, not bound to a device.

Alexa ecosystem products — including Sous Chef, Weather, Prime Video and others — are delivered across all three tiers.

02 · Two variants, two service sets

🧩 The call tier & the Alexa Lamp

The same assistant shows up as two products. The entry variant is a plain audio call; the Alexa Lamp variant projects the experience into the room — each unlocking a different set of services.

📞
Physical tier · the call

Audio-first, on any phone

For users who are not yet online — a free or sponsored call, no reading, in their language.

🗣️ Voice AI assistance 🏛️ Free, tiered government services 🌦️ Weather reports 📰 News & audio entertainment
💡
Spatial tier · the Alexa Lamp

Voice + visual, into the room

For users ready for more — the lamp projects an ambient, edgeless interface you act on with your hands.

🗣️ Voice + visual interface 🪄 Immersive, projected experience 🛒 Broader Alexa services (Prime, shopping, smart home) 💼 Business + home windows (SaaS, Amazon Quick)

🔄 How it works — call in, services out

No app, no login. The user dials a number; everything else happens on the cloud and comes back as a spoken (or two-color-light) answer.

1📞 Dial the number›

On any basic phone — a normal call, in the user's own language.

2🗣️ Speak the request›

Speech-to-text turns the caller's words into a query; the device shows status in two colors of light.

3🧠 AI finds the answer›

A language model + the right SaaS service (weather, schemes, irrigation) resolve the request, scoped to what the user is allowed to see 🔒.

4🔊 Hear it back›

Amazon Polly speaks the answer aloud — the whole loop runs 24/7, no screen required.

The constraint that made it scalable: the Physical device speaks and shows two colors of light — nothing more. Two moves kept it expressive and on-brand.

🔲 Know more about brand identity ›

A pixelated Alexa logo that stays readable even rendered by a handful of LEDs — identity that degrades gracefully.

04 · Spatial interface, limitless screen

The Alexa Lamp — the interface beyond screen

The Spatial tier is the Alexa Lamp: a lamp-shaped object that projects an ambient, edgeless interface into the room, so work and home live in the user's space rather than trapped on a device.

🎨 CMF variants — inspired from a flower to a bee.

🚀 Applications

Virtual location for employee and for students

With the interface in the room, one assistant serves the whole family — and the learner:

💼 At home

  • 🖥️Link a laptop to the home screen as an extension display — no new hardware.
  • 📊Amazon Quick dashboards, QuickSight analytics & SaaS workflows move onto the big screen.
  • 🏠A home window beside work: chores, ordering, news, CCTV briefing & entertainment.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧The family's day never leaves view (Alexa & Alexa Plus).

🎓 For students

  • 🛏️A dorm lamp projects onto the wall, past the screen into the room.
  • 💭Visualize your thoughts and plans in the space around you.
  • 🎓Campus labs (College of Engineering, Pune) support AWS training & verification.
  • 📡Learning spills past the screen, into the environment.

🛠️ The technology and the mood

🛠️ Technology

  • 📽️Step 1 — projector: casts the interface onto any surface in the room.
  • 📡Infrared depth sensing: tracks the hand so users act on the projected image directly.
  • 🎙️On-device mic & speaker: voice in, spoken + visual out, same as the audio tier.
  • ☁️Wi-Fi + cloud link: the same SaaS layer (Amazon Quick, QuickSight) that powers the Digital tier.
Under the hood · AWS architecture

🏗️ Three tiers, one AWS backend

A phone call, a screen and a projected room all reach the same shared core: AWS Lambda orchestrates, Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova Micro) answers, and one catalog of SaaS apps serves every tier. On the spatial device, raw infrared and depth data never leaves the room. Only short event messages (presence, fall, hazard) reach the cloud.

05 · The business model

💰 The call is the channel. Apps are the business.

A catalog of SaaS apps — built from Amazon's startup-cluster tools (Amazon Quick is one example) — earns through subscription & revenue share. The Physical (audio) tier is sponsored; the real return is reach, accent & language data, and discovery of what services these users need.

📖 What we still need to validate ›

06 · Key takeaways

🎯 Three things to take away

  • 1️⃣Audio opens the service to everyone — voice reaches users on any phone, with no screen needed.
  • 2️⃣Each tier adds depth, not complexity — Physical, Digital & Spatial build on one another.
  • 3️⃣Digital & Spatial are powered by SaaS apps — Amazon Quick and countless start-ups are services waiting for monetization, equipping users in their daily ecosystem regardless of interface.
