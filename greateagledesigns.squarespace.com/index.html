Financial Allocation Review Dashboard

Financial managers reviewed thousands of allocation change requests quarterly, drilling through cryptic IDs in legacy software across multiple tabs. I redesign the review workflow to enable financial managers to make faster, confident decisions without switching tools or losing context resulting quick turn around to executive decisions.

Result

  • Review time reduced from 9.7 hours to 33 minutes per 1,000 IDs — 94% faster faster executive turnaround

  • Senior managers shifted from data-hunting to decision-making

  • Exclusions surfaced upfront reduced misallocation risk

  • Built-in audit trail accelerated compliance reviews

Result

  • Efficiency increased 20% across product suite in first 3 months

  • Real time data visibility instead of manual monthly reports

AI-Powered Cooking Assistant Device

We’ve all been there. “You’re ready to cook, and the one thing you need... is missing!”

Customers struggled to plan and manage cooking due to time-shortage and a busy lifestyle, grocery shopping, pantry unavailability and deciding what to cook were the major friction-points. Sous Chef offers distraction-free cooking experience. It learns from customers buying habits to curate shopping list depending on nutritional preferences. Unlike fragmented recipe apps, Chef keeps users focused and engaged with smart suggestions rather than endless searching.

  • Step-by-step e-learning experience while managing groceries, expenses, and pantry inventory.

  • Tested positively with 20+ users in pilot testing

  • Engineered within budget constraints for affordability of target audience

Transformed BMW's Workspace Into a Collaborative Innovation Hub

BMW needed to draw employees back post-COVID by making the office more desirable. I led the research and product development, redesigning the workspace to foster natural collaboration through modular meeting areas and pods, swing-style window seating with natural light, BMW-iconic inspired cafeteria elements, and custom modular furniture reflecting collaborative values. Strategic wayfinding and spatial design encourage spontaneous conversations and idea sharing.

Results:

  • Three concepts tested, approved and developed

  • Positive response from BMW’s employees

  • Successfully balancing aesthetics, functionality, time and budget constraints, and human behavior to increase Innovation, productivity and workplace appeal for collaboration.

Sustainable Origami Chair for exhibitions

De-Clutter your space, De-Noise your mind

Bio-based polycarbonate from cornstarch addressing tariff tensions and plastic waste. It's origami inspired design folds into back rest and legs with minimal manufacturing complexity. It delivers 65% less carbon and energy than conventional plastic while eliminating 90% of waste economically. It replaces 1.4 billion in plastic imports creates 5000 jobs and generates 2.9 billion in domestic economic impact the transparent design suits offices, libraries and exhibitions. It taps into an 81 billion sustainable plastic market by 2030 positioning the US as a leader and domestic manufacturing and global sustainable exports.
